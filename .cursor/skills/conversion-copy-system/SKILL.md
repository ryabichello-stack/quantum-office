# Conversion Copy System

## Always read

`.agents/product-marketing.md`

## Invoke as needed

- `customer-research`, `copywriting`, `copy-editing`, `cro`, `pricing`, `offers`

## Rules

- Clarity > cleverness; benefits > feature dumps
- Specific numbers only from KB/docs (300 диалогов, 2 990 / 5 990 ₽, 30/100 мин)
- No AI clichés («революционный», «бесшовный», «it's not X it's Y»)
- Russian UI: natural spoken tone, not calques from English marketing

## CTA hierarchy

1. Try demo (voice / widget)
2. Lead form (demo on my business)
3. Human channels (phone, Telegram, email)

## Landing variants

- **`/`** canonical v2 — do not change hero without owner approval (`docs/P1.1_SITE_LANDING.md`)
- **`/v2`** conversion experiments — document in P1.1 when shipped
